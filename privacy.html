Jellyfall

A retro underwater arcade shooter for iPhone.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026.

Jellyfall does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Your best score and sound setting are saved only on your own device so the game can remember them. They never leave your device, and deleting the app removes them.

Because the game collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

How to play

Drag anywhere to steer your sub and keep a finger down to fire bubbles. Catch eggs from the gold jellies and tap the egg button to launch a smart bomb. Grab glowing capsules for power-ups, and watch out for the squid's tractor beam.
